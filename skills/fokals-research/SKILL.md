# Company and market research with Fokals

Fokals answers questions about companies and markets: what each company will likely adopt next, its health week by week, buying signals and feeds across all companies, the technology a company's website runs, the roles it hires for, its funding and announcements, and how industries, countries and vendors move.

## Before you spend

- Fokals charges per company returned, from the person's prepaid balance. Counting (`count_companies`), naming a company (`identify_company`) and listing filter values (`list_filters`) are free.
- Count before a large search, and say what the answer would cost.
- A request above $0.25 comes back with its price instead of running. Tell the person the price, and call again with `max_usd` only once they agree.
- A company not found costs nothing, and a company the account paid for is free to it again for 30 days.

## Which tool for which question

- **What will a company adopt next:** `likely_next` with the person's companies, or with a technology to get its likeliest next buyers.
- **Is an account healthy:** `company_health` gives a weekly band from signs of distress to breaking out, two scores and the reasons.
- **What is new across all companies:** `signal_feed`, `announcement_feed` and `change_feed`, from any day on, a page at a time.
- **How a market moves:** `market_trends` for a measure over time, `market_movers` for what moved most on a day, `market_breakdown` to split companies by an attribute.
- **Find companies:** `find_companies` by technology, buying topic, hiring, industry, country and more; `find_companies_by_description` from a description in plain words.
- **The person's own list:** `enrich_companies`, then `company_profile` for one company in depth.

## In the answer

- Name the companies and the dates the facts carry; never fill a gap with a guess.
- Say what the call cost, from the answer's `charged_usd`.
- A likelihood or a health band is an estimate. Give it with the reasons Fokals returns.
